# Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Delay Intelligence

> **Research-validated Decision Intelligence Prototype with a Production Roadmap**

This repository is a **research/demo prototype**, not an enterprise production system. It combines leakage-safe temporal delay prediction, calibrated probability, conditional delay-severity quantiles, conformal uncertainty, local SHAP explanations, drift research, and human-governed scenario recommendations for public-health supply-chain use cases.

## Evidence labels used throughout the demo

- **REAL DATA** — historical SCMS records contained in the bundled source dataset.
- **MODEL OUTPUT** — predictions, calibrated probabilities, conditional severity intervals, and SHAP values generated by fitted models.
- **SIMULATED SCENARIO** — recommendations, intervention efficacy assumptions, counterfactual states, and financial impact estimates.
- **EXPLORATORY ONLY** — legacy PC/Fisher-Z causal-discovery edges; hypotheses, not identified causal effects.
- **NOT VALIDATED** — DataCo/Olist portability adapters; no empirical external-validation metrics are claimed.

The authoritative interpretation of all metrics is in **`docs/FINAL_RESULTS_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`**.

## Current v2 serving path

The live FastAPI demo no longer uses the original proxy/mock serving chain. `artifacts/model_registry/v2` contains:

- CatBoost binary delay classifier trained on the temporally earlier training partition.
- Isotonic probability calibration fitted on a later, separate temporal calibration window.
- A calibrated decision threshold selected on that calibration window.
- LightGBM q05/q50/q95 models for **delay days conditional on a shipment actually being late**.
- 90% split Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR) adjustment.
- Real local CatBoost SHAP values at inference time.

The final 365-day holdout remains outside fitting, probability calibration, threshold selection, and CQR calibration.

## Historical research artifacts

Older research-stage artifacts are retained for provenance. They are **not silently overwritten** by v2. For example, the original packaged final-holdout classifier artifact reported PR-AUC 0.1810 and recall 3.28%. The v2 serving registry is a newly rebuilt truthful serving chain and has its own isolated holdout validation. See the source-of-truth document for the distinction.

Adaptive conformal, cost-sensitive, digital-twin, and counterfactual artifacts remain research/simulation modules. Their numbers are not realized production outcomes. In particular, the often-cited **$31,489.44** is a **scenario-based estimated economic impact** under the base cost assumptions at a 10% review budget; it is not audited or realized savings.

## External validation status

DataCo and Olist adapters are portability prototypes only. The previous hard-coded transfer metrics were retired. The executable cross-domain evaluator now refuses to fabricate zero-shot/recalibration results. See `docs/cross_sector_results.md`.

## Exploratory causal status

Legacy PC/Fisher-Z graph outputs are retained as hypothesis-generation artifacts only. Because encoded categorical variables were used, the project does not claim identified causal effects or intervention efficacy from those edges. See `docs/causal_discovery_report.md`.

## Production roadmap

Before production use, the project requires recent prospective operational data, real ERP/TMS/carrier/IoT integrations, target-domain external validation, security/authentication, observability/SLOs, governance approval, and prospective measurement of intervention outcomes and financial impact.

## Scientific guardrail

Historical SCMS records do not contain randomized intervention assignments. Model predictions are associations; SHAP values explain model behavior; exploratory graph edges are hypotheses; and simulated recommendations/cost impacts are not causal or financial guarantees.
