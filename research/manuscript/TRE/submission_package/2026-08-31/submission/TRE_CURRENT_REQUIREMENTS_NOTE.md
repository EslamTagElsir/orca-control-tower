# Current TRE / Elsevier Requirements Note (31 August 2026)

## Verified for this preparation cycle

- Transportation Research Part E specializes in logistics and covers Operations Management / Supply Chain Management topics including risk, disruption, artificial intelligence and machine-learning methods.
- Elsevier requires disclosure of substantive generative-AI / AI-assisted use in manuscript preparation; AI tools cannot be listed as authors and human authors remain responsible for the manuscript.

## Re-check in the live submission portal

The following are intentionally marked as portal-time checks because journal instructions can change:

- review model and exact title-page/anonymized-file handling;
- abstract word limit;
- keyword count;
- highlight count / character limits;
- graphical abstract requirement or optional status;
- accepted file formats / naming conventions;
- data/code repository fields;
- CRediT and conflict-of-interest portal forms.

The package therefore keeps the manuscript, title metadata, highlights, supplementary material, bibliography, declarations, and AI disclosure as separate editable components rather than hard-coding portal assumptions.
