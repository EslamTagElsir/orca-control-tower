# Current TRE / Elsevier Requirements Note (31 August 2026)

## Officially verified

- Transportation Research Part E explicitly specializes in logistics and includes Operations Management / Supply Chain Management topics such as risk, disruption and artificial intelligence. Elsevier also explicitly lists machine learning and artificial intelligence among acceptable research methods.
- Elsevier's journal AI policy (updated June 2026) requires a separate disclosure for substantive use of generative AI / AI-assisted tools in manuscript preparation. The declaration should identify the tool/service and purpose, state that authors reviewed/edited the content, and confirm author responsibility. AI tools cannot be authors.

## Must be re-checked in the live submission portal

The automated environment could not retrieve TRE's live Guide for Authors page because ScienceDirect returned a 403 response. The package therefore treats the following as **VERIFY AT SUBMISSION** rather than immutable requirements:

- double-anonymized review workflow and exact title-page/anonymized-file handling;
- final abstract word limit;
- exact keyword count;
- highlight count / character limit;
- graphical abstract requirement or optional status;
- exact file formats and naming conventions;
- data/code repository field requirements;
- CRediT and conflict-of-interest portal forms.

The package is intentionally conservative: it includes an anonymized manuscript, separate title-page template, five short highlights, editable source, figures, supplementary material, cover-letter template, declarations template, and AI-disclosure draft.
